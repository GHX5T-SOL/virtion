# Virtion Platform Roadmap

Virtion is education-first today: synthetic cases, simulated patients, and AI-assisted debriefs for medical training. It is not clinical advice and it does not use real patient data in the demo product.

## 1. Clinical Learning OS

- Expand the polyclinic case library across medicine, surgery, pediatrics, psychiatry, emergency care, and procedural skills.
- Keep the core loop short: brief, consult, investigate, diagnose, prescribe, safety-net, debrief.
- Use premium AI models for attending-level feedback, with provider fallbacks and deterministic rubric scoring when upstream systems fail.
- Preserve citation discipline: guideline-backed criteria should resolve to known recommendations instead of invented sources.

## 2. Immersive Clients

- Mobile app: daily OSCE drills, voice cases, flash debriefs, and offline deterministic rubric review.
- Desktop app: higher-fidelity 3D clinic, richer voice controls, multi-window study dashboards, and local node management.
- AR app: bedside overlays, anatomy callouts, simulated patient communication drills.
- VR app: immersive wards, ED triage bays, procedural rooms, and team-based simulations.

## 3. Decentralized Compute Mesh

Virtion can eventually ship opt-in node clients that contribute idle compute to biomedical workloads. The learning product becomes the distribution engine; the compute layer becomes the research substrate.

Candidate workloads:

- Protein folding and molecular docking simulations.
- Gene-sequencing analysis pipelines.
- Synthetic clinical case generation and validation runs.
- Medical AI benchmark/evaluation jobs.

Guardrails:

- Explicit opt-in consent.
- Clear device resource controls.
- No hidden mining, no covert telemetry, no patient data collection without consent and governance.
- Sandboxed workloads and transparent job history.

## 4. Consent-First Data and Future Medical AI

Future patient-data, diagnostic-support, and robot-doctor research must be treated as regulated, consent-driven R&D. Public product copy should frame these as future research directions, not current clinical claims.

Requirements before any real-world clinical data path:

- Consent, revocation, and audit trails.
- De-identification and governance review.
- Clinical validation, safety monitoring, and regulatory assessment.
- Human clinical oversight and clear boundaries between training, research, and care.

## 5. Media Suite Direction

The web app now uses a procedural ambient intro and cinematic launch surface. Future media work can add a Remotion or HyperFrames package that renders:

- 8-second launch ident.
- 20-second investor demo intro.
- 60-second product trailer.
